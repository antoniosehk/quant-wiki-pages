# Activities and Capstone

No coding is required. Predict first, calculate with units, and use a visualization to challenge your answer. Everything below stays in this browser unless you export it.

## Three short activities

1. Use expected value and trading costs to decide whether a strategy retains an edge.
2. Compare portfolio risk under two different correlations.
3. Draw a train/test timeline and mark any future information.
